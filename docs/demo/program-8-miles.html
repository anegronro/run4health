8 miles in 8 weeks

For runners who already cover 10K. Eight weeks to 8 miles (12.9 km), with a long run that goes past race distance.

Miles of measured running per week — peak 16.0 mi (25.7 km). Warm-ups and cool-downs are timed rather than measured, so they are not counted here. The dips are the down weeks, on purpose.

Guide

Entry requirement: run 6 miles (10K) without stopping. If you're not there yet, do the 10K program first.

Four runs a week: intervals on Tuesday, an easy run on Wednesday, tempo on Thursday and the long run on Saturday. Monday, Friday and Sunday are rest. That layout keeps a day between the two hard sessions and never puts the long run straight after one.

80 % of the mileage goes slow. Running the easy days too fast is the most common mistake, and it leaves you with no legs on the days that matter.

Week 4 is a down week and week 7 peaks above race distance on purpose. Don't skip either: the first is where the work sinks in, the second is what makes 8 miles feel manageable.

Distances here are in miles; the interval repeats are in metres, the way a track is marked. Effort guide (RPE): 3-4 you can hold a conversation; 7 you speak in three- or four-word bursts; 8-9 single words only.
