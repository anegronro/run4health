Miles of measured running per week — peak 14.2 mi (22.8 km). Warm-ups and cool-downs are timed rather than measured, so they are not counted here. The dips are the down weeks, on purpose.

Guide

Entry requirement: run 5 km non-stop, reasonably comfortably. If you're not there yet, do the 5K program first.

Four runs a week: intervals on Tuesday, an easy run on Wednesday, tempo on Thursday and the long run on Saturday. Monday, Friday and Sunday are rest. That layout keeps a day between the two hard sessions and never puts the long run straight after one.

80 % of the mileage goes slow. This is the most common mistake: running the easy days too fast leaves you with no legs on the days that matter.

Down weeks lower the load on purpose. Don't skip them or move them forward — that's when the body absorbs the work.

If you miss a week, restart at the week before the one you were due, not where you left off. If you miss three or more, go back to the start of the block.

Effort guide (RPE): 3-4 you can hold a conversation; 7 you speak in three- or four-word bursts; 8-9 single words only.
