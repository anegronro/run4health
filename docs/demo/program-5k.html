Zero to 5K

Eight weeks of walk-run until you can run 5 km non-stop. No running background needed.

0 of 24 sessions Reset
Beginner 3 days/week Running shoesWatch or stopwatch

Guide

Three sessions a week — Monday, Wednesday and Friday — with a rest day in between. If your week doesn't fit that, move the days around, but never run two days back to back.

All the jogging is at conversational pace: if you can't get a full sentence out, you're going too fast. In this program running slow isn't a concession, it's the method.

If a week feels like a wall, repeat it before moving on. Losing a week costs you nothing; an injury costs you two months.

Diffuse muscle soreness the next day is normal. Sharp pain in a knee, shin or Achilles that shows up while running and gets worse is not: stop and let it rest.
