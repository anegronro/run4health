What run4health stores about you

Plain version, because you deserve to know before you sign up.

What we keep

  • Your name and email address — so the app knows who you are and your progress stays yours.
  • Your password, hashed — scrambled with scrypt so that even we cannot read it. Nobody here can see or recover your password; if you lose it, it gets replaced, not looked up.
  • Which sessions you have ticked off, and when.
  • A session token for each browser you sign in from, so you stay signed in.

What we don't keep

No location, no GPS traces, no heart rate, no health records, no device data, no analytics, no advertising identifiers. There are no third-party trackers on any page — the app loads nothing from anyone else's servers.

Where it lives

On one server rented from DigitalOcean in New York, in a single database file. It is backed up nightly, and the backups are kept for two weeks. Nothing is shared with, sold to, or sent to anybody.

Who can see it

You, and Angel, who runs the server and can read the database directly. Be aware that everyone here shares one password to open the app, so treat this as a private space among people who know each other rather than a vault.

Deleting it

Ask Angel and your account and everything attached to it is deleted from the database. It disappears from the backups within two weeks after that.
